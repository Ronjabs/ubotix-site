Questions

What is UBotix?

The robotics club under the Honor Society Organization of the School of Information Technology, University of Baguio. We build, program and compete.

How do I join the club?

Fill out our registration form.

Who can order from you?

Our merch and 3D printing are for the whole University of Baguio community. PCB fabrication is for students' electronics and robotics projects.

How do I order?

Order via our Facebook page or send us a direct message.

Where are you?

General Luna Road, Baguio City, 2600 Benguet, Philippines.

Curious about robotics?

You don't need to be an expert. Message us and we'll tell you how to get started.
