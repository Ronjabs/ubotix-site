About

Who we are

UBotix is the robotics club under the Honor Society Organization of the School of Information Technology at the University of Baguio.

Learning by building

We build, program, and foster teamwork by engaging students in robotics competitions from local to international level. Some of us are just starting out, others have been to the podium. Everyone has something to build.

In 2025 the team travelled to Romania to join the 13th Robotics Championship.

Beyond the competitions, we use what we've learned to help other students with things like 3D printing and basic PCB fabrication for their own projects.

Competing

Team training and active participation in robotics competitions and extracurricular activities for UB School of IT students.

May 5–7, 2025 · Oradea, Romania

13th Robotics Championship

In May 2025 the UBotix team travelled to Oradea, Romania, to compete in the 13th Robotics Championship. We entered four categories and brought home a Top 4 finish in the Freestyle competition.

It came after our ROBOlution 2024 competition at the University of Makati.
